iOS Posture App
Detect bad head posture from the AirPods Pro IMU and nudge gently — posture, water, walk. These are rough sketches to choose a direction, not final visuals.
How to read this board
Each row below is one decision (an "axis"). Within a row, every phone is a different way to solve that decision — they're alternatives, not steps. Read the italic note under each sketch for when it works and what it costs. Nothing here is locked; pick the bits you like from any frame and we combine them. Tip: toggle these notes off with the showNotes control to see the sketches clean.

Axis B
How to show "good vs bad" posture
The single visual that lives at the center of the home screen. It runs from literal (a tilting head) to abstract (a color you feel). You picked the head/neck icon — B1 is that; the rest are here so you can see what you're trading away.
B1 · Head/neck icon your pick
Aligned
Slouch
Instantly legible, even mid-task. The shape literally mirrors what your neck is doing. Easiest to "get" with zero onboarding.
B2 · Posture score ring
A "% of day spent aligned" number engineers will optimize. Great for a daily/streak feel — but a number is less glanceable than a shape, and can feel like a grade.
B3 · Ambient color / shape
calm
alert
No numbers, no judgement — just a hue and a size you feel. The most "invisible" option, pairs perfectly with A3. Risk: too vague to act on.
B4 · Tilt gauge (degrees)

Precise and tunable — shows the exact angle and where your "bad posture" line sits. The most engineer-friendly for calibration, but reads as a tool, not a companion.
